# One Comida per Momento per Día

A Día has at most one Comida per Momento. Confirming more food with the same Momento on that Día appends Renglones to that Comida; moving a Comida onto an occupied Momento joins them (destination Renglones first). Two sittings of breakfast are the same Comida — a capture batch is not a second Comida. Stored duplicates for the same Día and Momento join in the order they were recorded.
