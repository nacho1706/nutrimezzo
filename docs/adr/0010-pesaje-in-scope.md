# Body mass is in as Pesaje only

ADR-0003 put body weight out of the MVP together with fasting, photo, barcode, and health-platform sync. This reopens that decision for body mass alone: Pesaje is one kilogram reading per Día, replace on save, not a Meta and not a Cumplimiento. Fasting stays copy (a morning hint), not a tracked product. Photo, barcode, health-platform sync, and the rest of ADR-0003 stay out.
